On LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → the full archive. Drop the zip here, or just Positions.csv (plus Education.csv, Skills.csv, Certifications.csv, Profile.csv if you have them).

PDF, Word (.docx) or plain text. The text is read in your browser; the structure (roles, dates, highlights) is extracted by Claude with your consent, and you check the result before anything is saved. Without that, a simpler date-pattern parser has a go and you fix the rest.

How this works

Cards. One card per role, oldest to newest, like a career in a card set. Click a card to open it large, back side up so the details are right there; click again to turn it over. "By team" gathers consecutive roles at one company into a stack; click a stack to spread it.

Free agent. When no role is current you're a free agent. The day count in the Free agency row runs from your layoff event if you've added one, otherwise from the end of your last role. Add a job-hunt event (+ Add → Event) as you apply and interview; the free-agent card and the timeline pick them up.

Timeline. Company spans sit above the line, job-hunt events sit on it. Drag to pan; pinch, Ctrl+scroll or double-click to zoom. "Career" fits everything, "Free agency" fits the job hunt.

Import. LinkedIn's data export (zip or CSVs), a resume (PDF, Word, text), or the job-hunt tracker spreadsheet. Every import shows a preview you can correct before saving.

Share. Builds a standalone career page with the job hunt left out.

Saving. Automatic, in this browser. Use Backup for a file you can move.
